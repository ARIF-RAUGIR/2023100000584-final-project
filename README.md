## Methodology

The assessment followed a structured penetration testing methodology covering five phases:

1. **Reconnaissance** — Active and passive information gathering including port scanning, DNS enumeration, and service fingerprinting.
2. **Enumeration** — Deeper investigation of discovered services to identify versions, configurations, and potential entry points.
3. **Vulnerability Assessment** — Analysis of enumerated services and web application endpoints to identify known vulnerabilities and misconfigurations.
4. **Controlled Exploitation** — Exploitation of identified vulnerabilities within authorized TryHackMe environments only, to validate impact and demonstrate risk.
5. **Documentation** — Recording of findings, evidence (screenshots), security impact, and actionable remediation guidance.
